# Pricing — OJEET Tracker

> OJEET Tracker is 100% free forever with zero paywalls, zero advertisements, and no premium tiers.

OJEET Tracker (https://tracker.ojeet.tech) is an offline-first JEE and NEET preparation workspace, syllabus tracker, and study planner designed for aspirants preparing for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and NEET UG.

---

## Free Forever Tier

- **Price**: $0 / ₹0 (100% Free Forever)
- **Paywalls**: Zero — every feature, analytics tool, timer, and syllabus tracker is fully unlocked for all users.
- **Advertisements**: None — completely distraction-free, privacy-respecting environment without promotional popups or sponsored trackers.
- **Account Requirement**: None — fully usable offline without registration.
- **Data Ownership**: 100% local — your progress data is stored directly in browser LocalStorage.

### Included Features & Capabilities

- **2026 Syllabus Tracking**:
  - Full chapter and subtopic tracking across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (JEE), and Biology (NEET).
  - Status indicators for NCERT exercises, PYQs (Previous Years' Questions), and coaching modules.
  - 30-day revision reminders and stale topic alerts.
  - Subtopic-level progress calculation (checked, unchecked, indeterminate).

- **Daily & Weekly Study Planner**:
  - Drag-and-drop weekly timetable paired with a comprehensive monthly calendar.
  - Auto-rescheduling: incomplete tasks automatically roll forward to Today with zero scheduling debt.
  - Exam milestone countdowns (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET UG).

- **Study Clock & Focus Stopwatch**:
  - Exam-mode focus timer, stopwatch, and customizable Pomodoro intervals.
  - Subject tagging and detailed session logging.
  - Ambient study tracks and focus soundscapes.

- **Mock Test Score Analytics**:
  - Dedicated score entry and analytics for JEE Main (PCM), JEE Advanced (PCM), and NEET UG (PCB).
  - Subject score breakdowns, percentile tracking, and trend charts.

- **Offline-First & Cloud Sync**:
  - Instant offline responsiveness with LocalStorage as the source of truth.
  - Optional cloud backup and multi-device sync via Supabase (Google Auth or Email/Password).
  - LZ-compressed, chunked payloads for minimal bandwidth consumption.

- **Visual Customization & Design**:
  - High-performance glassmorphism, dark-solid, and light themes.
  - Shareable progress cards with custom avatars.
  - Fully responsive, mobile-first design.
